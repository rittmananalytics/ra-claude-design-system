Who is Carwow?

  • UK car marketplace — consumers find, configure and purchase new and used cars; dealers and manufacturers pay for listings and leads.
  • Core products: Carwow marketplace (consumer listings + lead-gen) and GetYourCar (finance and subscription product).
  • ~400 people, London HQ. Also operating in Germany — separate GCP project, in scope for this engagement.
  • Analytics team of ~10, led by Becky Allsop (Director of Analytics & Data Science).
  • No dedicated analytics engineering function. Analysts have been building ad-hoc dbt models to answer individual questions; the gold layer has grown organically with significant redundancy and duplication across ~100 schemas.
