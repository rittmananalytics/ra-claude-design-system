Workshops · framing

What the domain workshops are (and are not).

Workshops are design sessions for the new gold layer. The question in every one: what are the canonical entities in this domain, how do they relate, and what are the authoritative KPI definitions? Participants are domain experts — Steph, leads, analysts who built the models — not end-user stakeholders articulating dashboard wishes.

In scope

  • Canonical entity definitions — what is an Order, User, Vehicle, Dealer, Lead in this domain
  • Existing join keys — what actually joins in source data vs what is worked around with aggregation
  • Conformed dimensions: User, Vehicle, Dealer, Date, Country, Channel
  • KPI definitions — the single authoritative definition of each metric this domain owns
  • Entity resolution problems — document explicitly as Phase 2 design inputs; do not attempt to solve in this sprint

Not in scope

  • "What dashboards do you want?" — that's Phase 2 requirements work
  • Ingestion or pipeline design
  • Anything requiring remodelling of source data or transformation logic
  • Agreeing new joins that have no basis in the existing source data

The tone that will not work: facilitated wishlist sessions. Steph has flagged this explicitly. If a session drifts toward "what would you like to see in reports," redirect to "what does this entity represent and how does it connect to that one."
