Delivery Team Strategy

Make delivery predictable.

Sprints finishing on time is not the same as a project being on track. From Q2 we manage delivery at two levels — sprint and milestone — and every engagement has a defined schedule from day one.

1

Every engagement has a milestone schedule

Defined within 10 days of kickoff. If the client hasn't provided one, we propose one. First deliverable agreed at the same time — small enough to ship fast, meaningful enough to matter.

2

Manage sprints and epics — not just sprints

Track health at both levels. A project is green when the milestone is intact, not just because last week's sprint closed. T&M and fixed-price are managed differently; neither runs itself.

3

Red for 14 days needs a recovery plan

Any engagement red for 14+ consecutive days without a written recovery plan is a delivery failure. The plan needs honesty about the cause, a specific action, and a named owner.
