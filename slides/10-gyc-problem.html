The hard design question

The GetYourCar problem. Know this before you walk in.

The most important single design question for the sprint. Raise it explicitly in the Tuesday GYC sessions.

The problem

A customer can submit a GYC enquiry without completing a configuration. Configurations and enquiries do not have a 1:1 join relationship. No reliable common join key exists in source data.

Why it matters

The metric the entire business wants is configuration-to-enquiry conversion rate. Currently impossible at transaction level. Workaround is an aggregated table (dealership × product × day) which avoids the join but is use-case-specific.

What Phase 1 does

Nothing. The aggregated table lifts as-is to BigQuery. Steph's Looker sits on it in its current form.

What discovery must produce

  • A clear written articulation of the entity relationship problem — what it is, why it exists
  • Investigation of whether a join key could be derived from source data — session IDs, event timestamps, probabilistic matching
  • A proposed entity model for Phase 2 — even if Phase 2 requires ingestion changes to implement, document what right looks like
Do not leave the Tuesday sessions without surfacing and documenting this. Ask directly:

"How do you currently compute configuration-to-enquiry conversion rate, and what table does that come from?"
